ℹ️ About McLean Trade Bot

Signals are generated using RSI, MACD, EMA50/200, support/resistance, volume confirmation, and a linear-regression AI trend model. Scans run every 30 minutes during market hours. BUY signals are automatically suppressed when the broader market (SPY/NIFTY) is down more than 1.5%.

⚠️ Not financial advice. Always do your own research before making investment decisions. Past signals do not guarantee future results.
